4. Rules that prevent damage

Speaker only on SPK + / SPK −Never connect a speaker wire to 3V, 5V or GND: it shorts the supply through the coil.
Mic on 3.3 V onlyThe INMP441 is a 3.3 V part; 5 V can destroy it.
Solder, don't twistTwisted joints work on the desk and fail when the hinge moves.
Battery to BAT padsA 3.7 V LiPo goes to the XIAO's BAT pads on the back, never to the 3V3 pin.
Unplug before rewiringRemove the USB cable before soldering or moving any wire.
